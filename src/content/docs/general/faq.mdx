common questions about don't feed machines, the sectors, and the tools.

## General

<details>
<summary>what is don't feed machines?</summary>

DFM is a constellation of sites, protocols, and open-source tools for people who make things and don't want those things scraped into training sets, alongside counter-surveillance and practical non-ai utilities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>is this legal?</summary>

everything in the DFM wiki is published for educational and defensive purposes. local laws vary on counter-surveillance hardware and radio-frequency equipment; you are responsible for knowing and following the rules where you live.

</details>

<details>
<summary>why are the tools and wiki open?</summary>

the site source code and tools are licensed under Apache-2.0, the wiki articles under CC BY-SA 4.0, and [The Leash](/ecosystem/sectors/leash/) consent corpus under CC0-1.0 so everything stays inspectable, auditable, and usable offline.

</details>

## Technical & Contributing

<details>
<summary>can i run the wiki and tools offline?</summary>

yes. the wiki builds to static files with Pagefind search built in, and standalone tools in [DFM Tools](/ecosystem/stations/dfm-tools/) work from disk or a local static file server.

</details>

<details>
<summary>can i contribute?</summary>

bug reports and fixes for site code, layout, accessibility, or build errors are welcome. wiki and sector prose is written and maintained by one person and is not open to pull requests. member-written walkthroughs and guides can be submitted at [learn.dontfeedmachines.com](https://learn.dontfeedmachines.com).

</details>
