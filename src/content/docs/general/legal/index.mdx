## OVERVIEW

`legal.dontfeedmachines.com` is the static legal store for the whole ecosystem: the HumanLayer Content License, the HL Covenant, Apache-2.0 / CC0-1.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 texts, privacy policy, terms of service, `ai.txt`, and `NOTICE`.

legal text is the one place you want the fewest moving parts, so it is served as plain files with no build step and no client scripts. the wiki also mirrors the core legal summaries under [Legal Center](/general/legal/).
