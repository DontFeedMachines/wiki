step-by-step walkthroughs for physical hardware builds, bills of materials, assembly, and verification.

## planned walkthroughs

1. **soldering basics:** safely soldering custom PCBs and through-hole/SMD components.
2. **flashing custom firmware:** flashing open-source firmware onto ESP32 and RP2040 boards (see [Firmware Overview](/guides/toolkit/firmware/)).
3. **deploying an acoustic defeater:** positioning and tuning ultrasonic microphone jammers.
4. **conducting an RF sweep:** using SDRs and handheld sweepers to locate hidden trackers.
