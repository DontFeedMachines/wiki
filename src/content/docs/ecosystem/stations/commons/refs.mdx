## Overview

the refs station is a members-only reference library, made & kept by people. every frame carries a source, a credit, and a license. curators add frames, members search them, circle them into pools, and inspect them on a light table. no generated images, no public viewing, and no general hosting.

## What It's for

a shared visual library across the sectors, searchable by tag. search results render as a contact sheet on black film strips with frame numbers printed in amber: hover a frame for the loupe, press `S` to circle it into a pool, or open it on the light table with the caption strip underneath.

## How It's Built

Astro + Cloudflare Workers (`refs.humanlayer.vip`), custom-built for the contact-sheet workflow with zero third-party origins.
