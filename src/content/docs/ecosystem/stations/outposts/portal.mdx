## Overview

`portal.dontfeedmachines.com` is the holding station for sector domains that are built locally or under construction and not yet wired to their final origin. parked domains attach as custom domains on Cloudflare Pages or route through bulk redirects (`redirects.csv`).

## How It's Built

plain static html and Cloudflare Pages Functions (`public/` and `functions/`), deployed with Wrangler.
