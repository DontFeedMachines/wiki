the help station is a custom ticketed inbox for `help.dontfeedmachines.com`, where a person reads what comes in and a person answers. no bot, no auto-reply, no ticket robot pretending to be helpful.

## What It's for

a single place for the mail that reaches the project: general help, licensing questions, and platform-setting tips that come in from [StopTheScrape](/ecosystem/sectors/stopthescrape/)'s crosswalks. mail to `help@` becomes a ticket, staff answer from `/staff`, and the answer goes out as email. a public form makes the same tickets behind a short proof of work.

## How It's Wired

Cloudflare Workers + D1 + TypeScript (`help.dontfeedmachines.com`). locally, it runs against a local D1 file under `.wrangler/` with no external network dependencies.
