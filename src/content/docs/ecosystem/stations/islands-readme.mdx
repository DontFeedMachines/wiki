## Protocols

where the sites & stations are places, the protocols can be seen as interconnective systems running through them, carrying bit & pieces from different sectors & stations and embedding them in shared tools/instruments, frameworks, packages, libraries, and more. how a page proves a person made it, how DFM records & grades the companies that take human work, how a lab that wants to comply finally can, and the rules for DFM ever training a model of its own.
