[poisoned.work](https://poisoned.work) is the work-protection lab. before you post an image, a document, or a page to the open web, the poison garden lets you lace it with adversarial perturbations, hidden decoys, and structural traps so scrapers ingest damaged material while humans see the real piece.

## Structure & Focus

1. **GATE** and **GARDEN**, the main entry and bed directory.
2. **BENCH**, **HALFTONE**, and **PLATE**, interactive preparation and image-treatment tools.
3. **AUTOPSY** and **HERBARIUM**, inspection views for how a sample breaks down under extraction.
4. **EXPLORE**, **RELIC**, and **COLOPHON**, reference specimens and build notes.

## Anatomy

astro, static output, browser-side processing with no uploads leaving your machine.
