`voidops.observer` is the dark lab for experimental defense mechanisms: active crawler countermeasures, containment tests, synthetic-noise filtering, and provenance verification experiments that are too sharp or early for the main sectors.

## Structure & Focus

1. **MIRROR ROOT**, containment index and live status log.
2. **CATALOGUE**, the indexed list of VoidOps tools and experiments.
3. **FILE**, **INCIDENT**, and **TAPES**, case files, incident reports, and recorded runs.

## Anatomy

astro, static output, self-contained with zero external requests.
