[s-w-e-e-t.spot](https://s-w-e-e-t.spot) is the crawler tarpit and honeypot sector: "a candy shop for crawlers." where [StopTheScrape](/ecosystem/sectors/stopthescrape/) names crawlers and hands out `403` configs, sweetspot catches the ones that ignore `robots.txt` and `ai.txt` and feeds them endless, plausible-looking decoy pages.

## Structure & Focus

1. **SHOP**, the decoy storefront and tarpit routes.
2. **TELLS**, how crawler behavior gives away automated clients.
3. **BUDGET** and **COLLATERAL**, crawl-budget exhaustion math and side-effect notes.
4. **GUESTBOOK**, visitor log.

## Anatomy

astro, static output, with a quiet entry option (`YES (quietly)`) that holds visual effects still.
