`flockbuster.org` is the automated license plate reader (ALPR) counter-surveillance sector, styled like a 1990s video rental store. it stocks the field guides, offline routing tools, and civic organizing kits to detect, route around, and dismantle municipal plate camera networks.

## Structure & Focus

1. **AISLES**, the full catalog of 23 titles organized by category.
2. **TAPE**, individual detail pages for each guide, tool, and routing kit.
3. **COUNTER**, check-out and quick-reference tools.
4. **NIGHT DROP**, field capture submission chute for logging camera sightings.
5. **CARD** and **BACKROOM**, membership card and staff/notes views.

## Anatomy

astro, static output, zero third-party origins or trackers.
